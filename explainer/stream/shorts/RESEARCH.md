# shorts/RESEARCH.md — how a one-minute Short is watched to the end and sends the viewer on

Researched 2026-11-18. Tags: **[O]** = YouTube/Google official, **[C]** = creator or third-party analysis, **UNVERIFIED** = no primary source found or sources conflict.

**Conclusion:** The feed treats the first second as the thumbnail and "viewed vs swiped away" as the click-through rate. Open on the claim with no greeting, keep one idea, pay it off by about 50 s, use the last 5 s to raise one question that only the live stream answers, and point down at the Related-video link. That link is the only clickable route from a Short to the live VOD.

## 1. Shorts mechanics (2025–2026)

- **Max length:** 3 min. This applies to square or vertical uploads made on or after 15 Oct 2024 [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en).
- **View counting:** since 31 Mar 2025 a Shorts view is every time "a Short starts to play or replay, with no minimum watch time"; the old metric became "Engaged views" [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9082582?hl=en). Since 24 Aug 2026 all formats count from the first frame. An engaged view is "watching past the initial seconds", and revenue, retention and average view duration still use it [O](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/). The seconds threshold is not published (UNVERIFIED).
- **How the feed judges a Short:** the feed shows no thumbnails, so the first views are "basically impressions" and "view vs swiped away" replaces click-through rate [O](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/renes-top-five-march-4-2024/). Studio calls it "How many chose to view" [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217?hl=en&co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts). A "seed audience" reaction decides whether reach grows or tapers off [O video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=388s).
- **Benchmarks:** YouTube publishes none. The best-known study (Galloway, 2023; 5,400 Shorts on 33 channels) found that Shorts under 60% viewed rarely did well and the best ones sat at 70–90%. Shorts with an average view duration over 50 s averaged 4.1M views [C](https://www.digitalinformationworld.com/2023/04/paddy-galloway-decoded-youtube-shorts.html). The study predates the 2025 change to view counting.
- **Replays:** average percentage viewed can go above 100% "because loops and replays count toward watch time" [C](https://prepublish.ai/guides/youtube-shorts-retention). There is no official confirmation, and YouTube has not said that loops boost distribution (UNVERIFIED).
- **Length for talk/edu clips:** no official ideal ("To what length do you need to tell your story?") [O video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=183s). Guides suggest 20–45 s for tips [C](https://1of10.com/blog/how-long-can-youtube-shorts-be/). Jenny Hoyos targets 34 s, because at 30 s or less a Short needed about 100% retention to go viral [C](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jayclouse_i-interviewed-jenny-hoyos-and-learned-a-ton-activity-7117486580600119296-QIS9). 50–60 s works only if retention holds.
- **Copyright:** Shorts longer than 1 min with an active Content ID claim are blocked [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en). Sources disagree about a relaxation from 24 Sep 2026 (UNVERIFIED). If the stream's background music might be claimed, stay at or under 60 s.
- **Originality update (1 Oct 2026):** YouTube now cuts reach for re-uploads of *other creators'* content. Long-form creators clipping their own videos should not be affected [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/470890423/prioritizing-original-content-on-shorts?hl=en), [C](https://www.androidauthority.com/youtube-shorts-promoting-original-content-reposts-3718668/).

## 2. The first 1–3 seconds

- **YouTube's advice:** "Start with a question, a surprising fact, or a visually captivating moment." Shorts are "often watched without sound, so captions are essential" [O](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/transitioning-your-long-form-content-to-youtube-shorts/). Grab attention "in the first few seconds" [O](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/your-guide-to-getting-started-with-youtube-shorts/).
- **Jenny Hoyos:** "you have one second to hook someone, especially on Shorts" [O](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/). Hook plus foreshadow is "2 lines… usually 3 seconds or less" [C](https://creatorscience.com/podcast/jenny-hoyos/) (quote from a search excerpt; page blocked).
- **Galloway:** "Treat your intro like a thumbnail" and make the first second punchy [C](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1646898373365051392).
- **Patterns for this show:** a bold claim from the book, a counter-intuitive fact, a cold open mid-sentence, the Paint drawing mid-stroke (visual oddity), and a hook line on screen at frame 0.
- **Avoid:** logos, intro graphics, greetings (YouTube's advice for the long-form start [O](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-related-videos-traffic-guide/)). Korean practitioners drop the channel intro and even "궁금하시죠?" and state the conclusion first (두괄식) [C](https://match.dropshot.io/blog/%EC%9C%A0%ED%8A%9C%EB%B8%8C-%EC%87%BC%EC%B8%A0-%EC%A1%B0%ED%9A%8C%EC%88%98-%EC%98%AC%EB%A6%AC%EB%8A%94-3%EA%B0%80%EC%A7%80-%ED%8C%81-6709).

## 3. Structure of a Short that is watched to the end

- **Hoyos's formula:** Hook, then Foreshadowing, then Narrative, then Ending with a twist [C](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jayclouse_i-interviewed-jenny-hoyos-and-learned-a-ton-activity-7117486580600119296-QIS9). Her ending rule: "Whatever you say you're gonna do, you end it right after you do it" [C](https://creatorscience.com/podcast/jenny-hoyos/).
- **One idea per Short:** use "single, actionable" points. Each Short should "be enjoyable on [its] own" [O](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/transitioning-your-long-form-content-to-youtube-shorts/). A clipped Short "must make sense without outside context" [O](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-related-videos-traffic-guide/).
- **Pacing:** guides say to re-hook every 3–5 s [C](https://www.theleap.co/blog/how-to-make-youtube-shorts-viral/). This is practitioner advice, not measured data (UNVERIFIED).
- **Captions (Korean):** Netflix's Korean guide: at most 16 characters per line, 2 lines, 12 characters per second for adults [O-Netflix](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/216001127-Korean-Timed-Text-Style-Guide). Shorts practice: white text, black outline, 1–2 lines [C](https://clip.dor.gg/ko/blog/gaming-shorts-viral-pattern). Font size: one project uses 84 px on 1080×1920 [C](https://github.com/JKPark83/short-printer/pull/10), a single example (UNVERIFIED).

## 4. Endings, open loops, series

- **YouTube's CTA advice:** put the CTA in the **final 5 seconds**. Say "tap the link for the full breakdown", point down at the link or flash the long video's thumbnail. Viewers click when they "expect immediate value" [O](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-related-videos-traffic-guide/).
- **Open loop:** "close one loop in part 1 and open the next one in its final line". A "follow for part 2" tease works "only if part 2 exists and is posted" [C](https://www.autoaishorts.com/blog/open-loops-in-short-videos).
- **Seamless loop:** if the last line runs into the first, replays add views [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9082582?hl=en), but engaged views exclude loops [C](https://fastclips.app/learn/youtube-view-counting-changes). A ranking benefit is UNVERIFIED.
- **Series:**
  - "Shorts series" launched 23 Sep 2026. It is limited to YouTube Partner Program channels and groups Shorts into seasons and episodes [C](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/youtubes-new-short-series-feature-brings-episodic-viewing-to-shorts/).
  - To set one up in Studio: Create → New show, or add show features to a playlist that contains only Shorts [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16590581?hl=en).
  - Without the Partner Program, use a normal playlist.

## 5. Linking to the long video

- **Related video:** one per Short, pointing to your own public or unlisted video. Viewers see "a clickable link below your channel handle". It requires advanced-feature access [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en).
  - Desktop steps: Studio → Content → pick the Short → Related video → Save.
  - It can point to a livestream [O](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-related-videos-traffic-guide/).
- **Edit into a Short:** this in-app tool auto-links the Short back to the source. It takes at most 60 s and only works on public long-form videos [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12836917?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid). Whether it works on live archives is UNVERIFIED.
- **Links in descriptions and comments:** still **not clickable**: "URLs placed in YouTube Shorts comments and Shorts descriptions are non-clickable". Hashtags and @mentions are clickable [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en).
- **Pinned comments:** not named there, but a pinned link is still a comment, so plain text [C](https://link.boo/guides/youtube-shorts-link-in-description). No official click metric for the Related-video link was found (UNVERIFIED).

## 6. 9:16 layout and safe zone

- YouTube publishes **no organic Shorts safe zone**.
- **Google's universal vertical ad template (1080×1920):** keep 288 px clear at the top, 672 px at the bottom, 48 px on the left and 192 px on the right. That leaves a safe box from x 48–888 and y 288–1248 (840×960) [O-PDF](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/universalsafezones-youtube.pdf). The pixel values were measured by third parties [C](https://gummbleads.com/tools/youtube-shorts-safe-zone/).
- **Organic Shorts UI, measured by third parties:** top 120–380 px, bottom 300–576 px, right rail 96–201 px [C](https://adchecklab.com/articles/youtube-shorts-safe-area-dimensions), [C](https://www.muzecmo.com/tools/safe-zones/youtube-shorts/).
- **Streamer clip layouts:** facecam on top at 40% with content below at 60%, or a 50/50 split [C](https://clypse.ai/blog/how-to-make-vertical-gaming-clips-facecam-2026). Captions go bottom-centre or between the two halves [C](https://blog.eklipse.gg/beginner-guide-2/twitch-clip-templates.html), [C](https://ovideo.io/resources/split-screen-video-maker).
- **16:9 source:** a landscape upload is never a Short [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en). At full width, 16:9 becomes a 1080×608 band, so crop into the Paint canvas to keep text readable.

## 7. Titles, hashtags, thumbnail, scheduling

- **Hashtags:** up to 3 of the "most engaging" appear by the title. More than 60 means all are ignored [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en). They are "not required" [O video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=293s).
- **Thumbnail:**
  - "99.9% of the views are coming from the Shorts feed", where no thumbnail shows [O](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/). So frame 0 is the real thumbnail.
  - Help says you pick a frame "only… from the YouTube app and not from Studio" [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10343433?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid).
  - Uploading a custom image on desktop is reportedly rolling out [C](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-custom-thumbnails/) (UNVERIFIED).
- **Titles:** no official Shorts title guidance was found (UNVERIFIED). Recommendation: use the hook phrase plus the book title.
- **Scheduling:** same as long videos: Schedule card (date, time, time zone) in Studio or the app; private until then [O](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1270709?hl=en). Posting time is "not something that we think about optimizing for" [O video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=327s). The mobile Shorts camera may lack the option; use Studio [C](https://www.lilachbullock.com/how-to-schedule-a-youtube-short/).

## 8. Korean conventions (키리누키, 버튜버 쇼츠, 책 쇼츠)

- **Terms:** 키리누키/클립 = a short cut from a stream; clip channels compete on speed, one-VTuber focus and subtitle quality [C](https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%A4%EB%A6%AC%EB%88%84%ED%82%A4).
- **Source credit:** agencies require the original stream's URL and title at the top of the description (Hololive, 2022) [C](https://nlab.itmedia.co.jp/nl/articles/2206/15/news209.html). On our own channel, the Related-video link plus the stream title in the description does the same job (recommendation).
- **Disliked:** clickbait thumbnails, exaggerated captions [C](https://www.fmkorea.com/4910348767).
- **Korean Shorts advice:**
  - conclusion first, no intro [C](https://match.dropshot.io/blog/%EC%9C%A0%ED%8A%9C%EB%B8%8C-%EC%87%BC%EC%B8%A0-%EC%A1%B0%ED%9A%8C%EC%88%98-%EC%98%AC%EB%A6%AC%EB%8A%94-3%EA%B0%80%EC%A7%80-%ED%8C%81-6709)
  - an opening sentence of about 15 characters [C](https://jederobranding.com/guides/youtube-shorts-automation/)
  - hook types: number, reversal, pain, secret, authority [C](https://alphacut.video/blog/shorts-hooking-strategy)
- **Book Shorts:** no Korean format guide for book Shorts was found (UNVERIFIED).
  - Market context: videos titled "북튜브" drew more than 350M views in H1 2024, per YouTube's own count [C-news](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20240812_0002846317).
  - 너진똑 stands out with animation and a witty tone [C-news](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20240812_0002846317).

## Workflow for a 50–60 s short from a long live stream

1. **Pick one standalone idea** (claim, surprising fact or reversal from the book) and note its timecodes; 2–3 Shorts per episode, different ideas.
2. **Write two lines first:** the hook (about 15 characters or fewer on screen) and the last line (the open question).
3. **0.0 s:** frame 0 shows the hook text and a moving drawing. No logo, greeting or fade.
4. **0–2 s:** the claim or question is spoken. Cut into the middle of the sentence if needed.
5. **2–5 s:** foreshadow what the viewer will get ("3가지", "왜 그런지").
6. **5–40 s:** 2–3 escalation beats. Change the visual every 3–5 s (zoom into the canvas, caption pop, avatar reaction). Cut ums and dead air.
7. **40–52 s:** payoff, delivering the promise.
8. **52–58 s (final 5 s):** open one new question the live stream answers, with the verbal CTA "전체 라이브는 아래 링크에서" while pointing down.
9. **Ending rule:** cut right after the last line, with no outro card. If possible, make the last line flow into the first.
10. **Captions:** at most 16 Korean characters per line, 2 lines, 12 characters per second or less, white text with a black outline.
11. **Layout (1080×1920):**
    - Strict safe box: x 48–888, y 288–1248. Keep captions and the hook in it.
    - Canvas crop at about y 300–900, captions at about y 920–1240.
    - Avatar below y 1250. Its face can sit down to about y 1530, the organic UI limit (UNVERIFIED).
    - Leave the right 192 px free of text. Drop the chat overlay.
12. **Publish:** Related video = the live VOD (Studio desktop); title = hook + book title; up to 3 hashtags; frame 0 as thumbnail (app); schedule; add to the "책듣는밤 Shorts" playlist or series.
13. **Review after 48 h:** compare viewed vs swiped away and the 0–3 s retention drop against your own Shorts median. Rewrite hooks first.
